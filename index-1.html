Need to know

Frequently asked questions

Are the prices on this page live?

No. Prices are intentionally left blank until the owner adds and verifies the current offers. Always confirm the final price before payment.

How do I place an order?

Choose a package and tap “Request this bundle.” The site will prepare a message. The owner must configure the correct WhatsApp contact number for the request to open a chat.

Is this the official MTN Ghana website?

No. Tommy Lee Data Hub is an independent data-offer page and is not affiliated with or endorsed by MTN Ghana.

How can I report a problem?

Contact the site owner using the verified support contact that the owner adds to this page.
